About Us

About Our School

Established in 1996, Gramin Shikshan Sanstha's English Medium School is a co-educational institution affiliated to the Maharashtra State Board, located in Saphale, Taluka Palghar, District Palghar, Maharashtra – 401102. For three decades the school has provided English-medium education to students from the local Saphale community, and is currently preparing for the Academic Year 2026–2027.
